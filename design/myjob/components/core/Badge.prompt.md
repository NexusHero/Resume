Small mono chip for skills, tags and counts.

```jsx
<Badge variant="soft">React</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid">Top-Match</Badge>
<Badge variant="glass">Remote</Badge>   {/* on the dark sidebar */}
```

- Light surfaces: `outline` (default), `subtle`, `solid` (accent fill), `soft` (accent tint).
- Dark shell: `glass` (translucent), `light` (white chip).
- For pipeline stages use **StatusBadge**, not Badge.
